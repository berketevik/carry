# Cedar Pilot Plan

Cedar is the internal name for the shared review workspace pilot. The pilot runs
with two teams and one read-only observer group.

## Delivery

Cedar pilot delivery is October 15, 2026. The date was fixed at the July
planning session and covers the pilot build only, not the general release.

## Checkpoints

1. Corpus import complete and reversible.
2. Review flow usable without editing configuration files.
3. Handover notes written by the pilot teams themselves.

## Out of scope

Mobile clients, external sharing and any billing work stay out of the pilot.
