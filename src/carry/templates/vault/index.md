# Vault

* [notes](notes/) - living knowledge; each note's kind is in its `type`
* [sources](sources/) - raw material: clips, transcripts, chat raws
* [log](log/) - the time stream: daily notes, outputs, items approved from chats
* [+](+/) - the inbox: everything lands here first
* [workbench](workbench/) - outputs of work an agent started on its own
* [x](x/) - templates and support files
